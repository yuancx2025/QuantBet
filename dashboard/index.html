What these numbers mean

Quarter margin d is team points minus opponent points in that quarter. Quarter-end lead c sums all regulation quarters to that break. They are distinct measures. Final results include all overtime.

Comeback = final wins among games trailing at halftime. Retention = final wins among games leading at halftime. Tied halftime is separate. Unavailable rates appear as —. Variability is sample standard deviation across games, with no inference or prediction.
